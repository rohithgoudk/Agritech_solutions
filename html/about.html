What we believe

Four rules we have never had to rewrite.

01

Measure before you argue

Opinions about a block are cheap and usually wrong. A sample, a probe and a weighbridge ticket settle it in an afternoon.

02

Take no margin on inputs

We sell advice and nothing else. No supplier pays us, so a recommendation is only ever about what the crop needs.

03

Publish the bad years

The close-out report goes out whether the season was kind or not. A wet July belongs in the record as much as a good harvest.

04

The same person, all season

The agronomist who walked your block in March is the one who answers the phone in August. No handovers, no account team.

We would rather turn a block down than take it on and give it half an agronomist.

Mara Ilić — Farm Manager, here since 1994
