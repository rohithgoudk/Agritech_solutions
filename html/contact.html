Before you write

Four things that
save a phone call.

Not compulsory — but if you have them to hand, the first answer gets a lot more specific.

  • The size and the cropHectares and what is in the ground. "84 ha of winter wheat" tells us more than a paragraph.
  • Your last soil testEven an old one. If there isn't one, say so — that is useful in itself.
  • Three seasons of yieldWeighbridge numbers if you have them, a decent estimate if you don't.
  • What is actually worrying youThe thing you would say to a neighbour over the gate. That is usually the real question.

Would rather just talk?

Ring the office. If Mara is in a block she will call you back the same day — she always does.
