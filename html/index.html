Field stories

A sense of the work.

Open a block to see how the season ended.

North Ridge had been getting one heavy nitrogen pass in March. We split it three ways against tissue tests and soil mineral nitrogen, and moved the last pass a fortnight later than the grower was used to.

  • Outcome+1.1 t/ha
  • Nitrogen used−12%
  • AgronomistMara Ilić

Hollow Creek was irrigating to a calendar. Probes at three depths and a forecast-driven schedule cut the number of passes while holding the crop through pod fill in the driest July on record here.

  • OutcomeYield held
  • Water used−18%
  • AgronomistTom Okafor

Eastmark was picking on a fixed date and losing fruit at both ends. Brix and firmness sampling moved the start four days and split the block into two passes, which the packhouse could actually absorb.

  • OutcomeGrade-out 96%
  • Rejects−31%
  • AgronomistElena Cho

Cold Spring had been in continuous cereals for nine years. Three seasons of a six-way cover mix, grazed rather than cut, lifted organic matter measurably and took a pass of cultivation out of the rotation.

  • Outcome+0.4pt OM
  • CultivationOne pass fewer
  • AgronomistPriya Nadar

Readings were being written on a clipboard and typed up on Fridays. Eighty probes and four weather stations later, the same decisions get made on Tuesday morning with two days of data behind them.

  • OutcomeLive across 8 blocks
  • Decision lag3 days → same day
  • AgronomistMarcus Ide
